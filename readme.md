# Beaufort

Beaufort does flashcards. Just like Quizlet, Anki, and every other person learning JavaScript. What Beaufort does differently is the creation part.

I'll admit that Beaufort isn't anything special in the studying aspect, but making/generating flashcards with Beaufort is a much more enjoyable experience than Anki.

First, you don't need an account to get started; Beaufort will never ask you for an email. All you need is a browser and a computer.

Secondly, you won't be swarmed by options like Quizlet does. I know that you went to Beaufort to make flashcards, so I'm going to hand you the tools to make flashcards up front. Other utilities are there, but they probably aren't you priority.

Third and finally, there's more than one way to do it. I designed Beaufort so you don't have to modify your notes provided by your Teacher or Professor. It's not perfect, but you don't have to split each flashcard in two unlike other tools.

As of right now, there are two ways to do it, with sub options for each of them:

1. Raw text

   For raw text, you can choose between two formats: `_answer_` or `ANSWER`. Beaufort accepts the first by default, but enabling the second is just a checkbox away.

3. Files

   Beaufort also accepts .txt files, but .md (Markdown) support is right around the corner.

It goes without saying, but Beaufort is 100% free and open source. No accounts, no sign ins of any kind, no registration. Something as simple as flashcards shouldn't be this hard. Beaufort makes them easy.
